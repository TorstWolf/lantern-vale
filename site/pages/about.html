A universe

TFU, Torstwolfs Fictional Universe, is a world of five continents, empires and nations, angels, local gods and anomalies. The codex collects everything that's canon so far.

The name

Why “Vayns Library”?

The name comes from TFU. Vayns Library is the Library Beyond Horizons, kept by the angel Lucian Vayn. It lies among sky islands, in a world his own mind created.

The motto on the logo says what this place is for: truth through reason.
