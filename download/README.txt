LANTERN VALE
A pixel-art idle RPG. Build a hero, hire a party, and follow the lantern across the island.

PLAY IN A BROWSER
Open "Lantern Vale.html". The whole game is inside that one file and works offline.
Progress saves automatically in that browser; another browser or cleared site data
starts a new journey.

PLAY ON ANDROID
Copy "Lantern Vale.apk" to an Android phone (Android 8.0 or newer), open it, and allow
installing apps from that source when asked. Progress is saved inside the app.

HOW TO PLAY
Create your hero; after that your party fights on its own, also while you are away.
The bottom bar switches between:
  Battle   the area you are in
  Hunt     pick any monster you have met to farm experience and materials
  Map      travel between areas; beat an area's last boss to open new places
  Village  the guild (quests, adventurers, training), the church (skills, class,
           race), your home (hero, gear, bag), the blacksmith, the market, and the
           campfire (meals, potions, rest)
The menu at the top right pauses the game, switches music and sound effects, and
starts a new journey.
